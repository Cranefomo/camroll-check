# iPhone privacy checklist (5 minutes)

do these on the phone itself. menu names are from iOS 18, they can shift a bit between versions.

## 1. search your own photos for text
iOS reads text inside photos (Live Text), so the Photos search finds it.

open Photos > Search and try, one by one:
`password` `пароль` `card` `CVV` `IBAN` `recovery` `seed` `backup codes` `passport` `PIN`

delete what shouldn't be there. then Albums > Recently Deleted > Select > Delete All.
(camroll-check does the same on a computer, for the whole library at once.)

## 2. which apps can see all your photos
Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos

anything on **Full Access** sees every photo, including those screenshots.
switch to **Limited Access** or **None** unless the app really needs your whole library.

## 3. camera, microphone, contacts
Settings > Privacy & Security > Camera / Microphone / Contacts
turn off anything you don't recognise or haven't used in months.

## 4. where your phone thinks you live
Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > System Services > Significant Locations
shows places you go often. clear history or turn it off if you don't use the features that need it.

while there: Location Services list, set apps to **While Using** or **Never**, not **Always**.

## 5. location in photos you send
when sharing a photo: tap **Options** at the top of the share sheet, turn off **Location**.

## 6. old Messages attachments
Settings > General > iPhone Storage > Messages > Review Large Attachments / Photos
and Settings > Apps > Messages > Keep Messages: 1 Year instead of Forever.

## 7. passwords
Settings > Passwords > Security Recommendations
shows reused and leaked passwords. fix the leaked ones first.
